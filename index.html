Studying for Jeopardy, one flashcard at a time.

Underneath the show sits a much quieter project: getting good enough to actually stand at a podium. I mined years of clues from the J-Archive — the fan-run database of every question Jeopardy has ever aired — and turned them into a custom Anki deck.
