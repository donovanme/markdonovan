TriviaBot

The upload tool behind the Instant Trivia YouTube channel.

TriviaBot is a small program that runs on my own computer. Once the day's trivia video is made, it signs in to YouTube with my Google account and does the publishing chores for me:

  • uploads the video and schedules when it goes public
  • sets the title, description and thumbnail
  • adds the video to the right playlist

It is not a public app or service. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it works on one channel only — mine. Google asks every app that connects to a Google account to say who runs it and how it handles data, which is why this page and the privacy policy exist.
